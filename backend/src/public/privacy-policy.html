Privacy Policy

High Street Gym ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use our management portal or interact with our gym.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the portal, we may collect:

  • Account and contact details, such as your name, email address, and login information.
  • Gym service details, such as bookings, session attendance, and activity preferences.
  • Information you submit through forms or other portal features.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the portal.

Please do not submit sensitive health or financial information through general text fields.

How We Use Information

We use personal information to manage accounts, provide gym services and bookings, respond to enquiries, maintain and secure the portal, and meet legal obligations. We will use information for other purposes only where permitted by law and, when required, with your consent.
